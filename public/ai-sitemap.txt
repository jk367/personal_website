ABOUT:
James grew up in NYC, studied economics, computer science, and sociology at Columbia and Sciences Po, and taught math in Tbilisi, Georgia for seven months in 2023. He returned to NYC in January 2024 and joined Faraday in November 2025 as Business Operations Manager. He still shoots black-and-white portraits, runs the stop1 party series in Brooklyn, and DJs under the name Kettle.
